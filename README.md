# SiteView360

Turn a 360° walk-through video into a Street View-style tour of your construction site. Everything is stored on your own computer.

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/tossi15-a11y/siteview360-releases/releases/latest)**

- **macOS** 13 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel: choose the `.dmg` file.
- **Windows** 10 or 11, 64-bit: choose the `Setup` `.exe` file. It is not yet code-signed, so SmartScreen may ask you to choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

- **Free:** one tour with up to 60 images, including map, notes, sharing, HTML export and PDF report.
- **SiteView360 Pro:** one-time purchase, no subscription. Unlimited tours and images, before/after comparison, and your own company name and logo. [Buy Pro](https://siteview360-lisens.vercel.app/api/kjop)

The app updates itself from this repository. More information: [anleggtech.no/programvare](https://anleggtech.no/programvare)
